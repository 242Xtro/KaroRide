# Working together

KaroRide is a small independent project for riders and camera enthusiasts.
Questions and contributions in English or German are welcome.

- Be respectful. Discuss the change or problem rather than attacking a person.
- Make room for beginners and different experience levels.
- Keep feedback concrete: camera model, app version, steps and expected behaviour.
- Do not post personal identifiers, credentials, private recordings or unredacted logs.
- No harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, spam or unsolicited advertising.

The maintainer may edit or remove content and limit participation when these
rules are violated. For a sensitive concern, use the contact in the legal notice
at https://www.hellmold.de/web/impressum/ rather than exposing details publicly.
This volunteer project does not guarantee response times.
